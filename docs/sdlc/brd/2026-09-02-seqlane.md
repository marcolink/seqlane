# Seqlane

## 1. Business Problem

AI coding agents can perform increasingly substantial engineering work, but recurring engineering activities still require significant manual orchestration by developers.

Teams repeatedly solve similar classes of problems—such as dependency-update failures, CI failures, migrations, reviews, and repository maintenance—without an effective way to capture and reuse the engineering process behind those solutions.

This leads to:

- repeated manual effort;
- inconsistent approaches to similar work;
- engineering knowledge remaining with individuals rather than becoming reusable organizational capability;
- limited ability to automate recurring engineering work end-to-end.

## 2. Business Opportunity

Seqlane provides a way to turn successful engineering practices into **reusable engineering automation**.

The goal is to enable teams to capture how recurring engineering work should be approached and make those practices easily reusable by individual engineers, repositories, and teams.

Over time, this should allow more routine engineering work to move from manually orchestrated AI assistance toward repeatable autonomous engineering workflows, while engineers retain control over what work is automated.

## 3. Business Objectives

### brd.seqlane-O01: Reduce repetitive engineering effort

Reduce the amount of developer time spent repeatedly orchestrating common engineering tasks.

### brd.seqlane-O02: Capture organizational engineering knowledge

Enable successful engineering practices to be expressed as reusable workflows and tasks rather than remaining implicit knowledge held by individual engineers.

### brd.seqlane-O03: Increase consistency

Make recurring engineering activities more consistent across engineers and repositories where a common approach is appropriate.

### brd.seqlane-O04: Enable greater automation

Allow suitable engineering work to be completed autonomously when the problem and expected outcome can be clearly bounded.

### brd.seqlane-O05: Encourage reuse

Make it easy for engineers and teams to build on existing automation rather than recreating similar solutions.

## 4. Primary Users

Seqlane primarily serves:

- software engineers using AI-assisted development;
- engineering teams maintaining repositories;
- engineers creating reusable development automation;
- platform and developer-productivity teams providing common engineering capabilities.

## 5. Initial Business Use Case

The initial reference use case is the remediation of **failed Renovate dependency updates**.

This represents the type of work Seqlane is intended to improve:

- recurring;
- time-consuming;
- often similar across occurrences;
- suitable for AI-assisted investigation and remediation;
- capable of being objectively verified.

The use case validates the broader business hypothesis rather than defining Seqlane as a Renovate-specific product.

## 6. Business Success

Seqlane will be successful if teams can take engineering processes that currently require repeated manual agent orchestration and turn them into reusable automation that meaningfully reduces developer effort.

Initial measures should focus on:

- reduction in manual intervention for suitable tasks;
- successful completion rate of automated engineering work;
- time saved compared with equivalent manual handling;
- reuse of automation across multiple occurrences, engineers, or repositories;
- adoption by engineering teams.

## 7. Business Scope

Seqlane is intended to support **software-engineering automation**.

It is not intended to become:

- a general business-process automation platform;
- a project-management system;
- a replacement for coding agents;
- a replacement for CI/CD systems.

## 8. Business Hypothesis

> **If engineering teams can capture recurring AI-assisted development practices as reusable automation, they can reduce repetitive work, increase consistency, and progressively automate a meaningful portion of routine software engineering.**
